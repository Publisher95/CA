\noindent{\bf Task 14} Suppose that $z_1,z_2,w \in \mathbb{C}$. Show that $w(z_1+z_2) = wz_1 + wz_2$.\\

$w*(z_1 + z_2) = (a*c - b*d + c*bi + a*di) + (a*e - b*f + e*bi + a*fi)$\\
